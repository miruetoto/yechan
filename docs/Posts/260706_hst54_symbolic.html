기호 약속: \(\implies\) 함의, \(\iff\) 동치, \(\therefore\) 결론, \(\because\) 근거, \(\land\) 그리고, \(:=\) 정의, \(\nearrow\) 비감소, \(\exists!\) 유일 존재, \(\overset{\text{X}}{\implies}\) “X에 의해”, \(\blacksquare\) 증명 끝.

서술 규약: 각 자체 증명과 그 안의 Step은 목표(Goal:)를 먼저 제시한 뒤, 여러 기호주의 state를 itemize로 차근차근 쌓아(미괄식) 목표에 이른다 — 기호주의가 미괄식과 잘 맞기 때문이다.

§5.4 The ergodic theorem and proof of the balanced theorem

The three hypotheses just verified are the drift (H3, §5.1), the petite set (H2, §5.2), and \(\psi\)-irreducibility with aperiodicity (H1, §5.3). These are the standing assumptions of four classical ergodic theorems of Meyn and Tweedie (2009), which we state in general form for a Markov chain \(\{Y_t\}\) on a state space \(\mathsf{Y}\) and then apply to the round skeleton. They are phrased through Harris recurrence, which we recall first.

Definition ((Positive) Harris recurrence and invariant law; cf. Meyn and Tweedie (2009), §9.1, §10.1)

A \(\psi\)-irreducible chain is Harris recurrent if for every \(A\) with \(\psi(A) > 0\) and every starting state \(y\) it visits \(A\) infinitely often almost surely: \(\mathbb{P}_y(Y_t \in A \text{ for infinitely many } t) = 1\). An invariant probability is a probability measure \(\pi\) with \(\pi(A) = \int_{\mathsf{Y}} P(y, A)\,\pi(dy)\) for all \(A\) (the law is unchanged by one step). The chain is positive Harris recurrent if it is Harris recurrent and admits an invariant probability \(\pi\); such a \(\pi\) is then unique.

Lemma 1 (Foster drift criterion for positive Harris recurrence; cf. Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 11.0.1, originating in Foster (1953))

Let \(\{Y_t\}_{t \geq 0}\) be a \(\psi\)-irreducible, aperiodic Markov chain on a state space \(\mathsf{Y}\), and suppose there exist a petite set \(C \subset \mathsf{Y}\), a measurable \(V \colon \mathsf{Y} \to [0, \infty)\), a constant \(\tilde b < \infty\), and a non-decreasing \(\varphi \colon [0, \infty) \to [0, \infty)\) with \(\varphi(x) \to \infty\) as \(x \to \infty\), such that

\[\mathbb{E}[V(Y_{t+1}) \mid Y_t = y] \;\leq\; V(y) - \varphi(V(y)) + \tilde b\,\mathbf{1}_C(y) \qquad \text{for all } y \in \mathsf{Y}.\]

Then \(\{Y_t\}\) is positive Harris recurrent with a unique invariant probability \(\pi\), independent of the initial distribution.

Proof. Since \(\varphi\) is non-decreasing with \(\varphi(x) \to \infty\), there is a level \(x_*\) with \(\varphi(x) \geq 1 + \tilde b\) for \(x > x_*\), so on \(\widetilde C := C \cup \{V \leq x_*\}\), which is petite as the union of the petite \(C\) with a sublevel set of \(V\), the drift reduces to the unit form \(\mathbb{E}[V(Y_{t+1}) \mid Y_t = y] \leq V(y) - 1 + \tilde b\,\mathbf{1}_{\widetilde C}(y)\). This is Foster’s drift criterion for positive Harris recurrence; see Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 11.0.1, originating in Foster (1953). \(\square\)

Lemma 2 (Stationary moment bound, the \(f\)-norm ergodic theorem; cf. Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 14.3.7)

Let \(\{Y_t\}_{t \geq 0}\) be positive Harris recurrent with invariant probability \(\pi\), and suppose that for measurable \(V \colon \mathsf{Y} \to [0, \infty)\) and \(f \colon \mathsf{Y} \to [0, \infty)\), a petite set \(C\), and a constant \(\tilde b < \infty\),

\[\mathbb{E}[V(Y_{t+1}) \mid Y_t = y] \;\leq\; V(y) - f(y) + \tilde b\,\mathbf{1}_C(y) \qquad \text{for all } y \in \mathsf{Y}.\]

Then the stationary mean of \(f\) is finite, \(\mathbb{E}_\pi[f] = \int_{\mathsf{Y}} f\,d\pi \leq \tilde b < \infty\).

Proof. This is the \(f\)-norm ergodic theorem, the stationary form of the comparison theorem; see Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 14.3.7. \(\square\)

Lemma 3 (Law of large numbers for positive Harris chains; cf. Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 17.1.7)

Let \(\{Y_t\}_{t \geq 0}\) be positive Harris recurrent with invariant probability \(\pi\). Then for every \(\pi\)-integrable \(g\),

\[\frac1t\sum_{k=0}^{t-1} g(Y_k) \;\xrightarrow{t \to \infty}\; \mathbb{E}_\pi[g] = \int_{\mathsf{Y}} g(y)\,\pi(dy) \quad \text{a.s.}\]

Proof. This is the law of large numbers for positive Harris recurrent chains; see Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 17.1.7. \(\square\)

Lemma 4 (Ratio limit theorem; cf. Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 17.3.2)

Let \(\{Y_t\}_{t \geq 0}\) be positive Harris recurrent with invariant probability \(\pi\). Then for all \(\pi\)-integrable \(f, h\) with \(\pi(h) \neq 0\),

\[\frac{\sum_{k=0}^{t-1} f(Y_k)}{\sum_{k=0}^{t-1} h(Y_k)} \;\xrightarrow{t \to \infty}\; \frac{\pi(f)}{\pi(h)} = \frac{\int_{\mathsf{Y}} f\,d\pi}{\int_{\mathsf{Y}} h\,d\pi} \quad \text{a.s.}\]

Proof. This is the ratio limit theorem for positive Harris recurrent chains; see Meyn and Tweedie (2009), Thm. 17.3.2. \(\square\)

Lemma 5 (skeleton-step lift)

Suppose the one-round skeleton \(P^{\star}\) is positive Harris recurrent with invariant probability \(\pi^{\star}\), and the round lengths are uniformly bounded, \(1 \leq m_r \leq D < \infty\). Then the cycle-occupation law

\[\pi(A) \;:=\; \frac{\mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}\bigl[\sum_{q=0}^{m_r-1}\mathbf{1}\{S_{t_r+q}\in A\}\bigr]}{\mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}[m_r]}\]

is a probability measure, invariant for the per-step chain \(\{S_t\}\). Moreover, every \(g \colon \mathsf{S} \to \mathbb{R}\) with \(\mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}\bigl[\sum_{q=0}^{m_r-1}|g(S_{t_r+q})|\bigr] < \infty\) \(\cdots\,(\ast)\) obeys the per-step law of large numbers \(\frac1t\sum_{k=0}^{t-1} g(S_k) \to \int_{\mathsf{S}} g\,d\pi\) a.s.

Proof. Goal: \(\ \dfrac1t\sum_{k<t}g(S_k)\xrightarrow{t\to\infty}\displaystyle\int_{\mathsf{S}}g\,d\pi\) a.s. \(\ \land\ \pi\ \text{= }\{S_t\}\text{의 }\exists!\,\text{불변확률}.\) 아래를 차근차근 쌓아 목표에 이른다.

  • (정의) \(m_r:=t_{r+1}-t_r\in[1,D]\), \(\ F_r:=\sum_{q=0}^{m_r-1}g(S_{t_r+q})\), \(\ h(s^{\star}):=\mathbb{E}_{s^{\star}}[m_r]\in[1,D]\), \(\ f(s^{\star}):=\mathbb{E}_{s^{\star}}[F_r]\).
  • (well-def) \((\ast)\iff\pi^{\star}(|f|)<\infty\), \(\ \pi^{\star}(h)\in[1,D]\ \implies\ \pi(g)=\dfrac{\pi^{\star}(f)}{\pi^{\star}(h)}\) 잘 정의됨.
  • (스켈레톤 비율) \(\{S^{\star}_r\}\ \text{양 Harris}(\pi^{\star})\land f,h\in L^1(\pi^{\star})\ \overset{\text{Lemma 4}}{\implies}\ \dfrac{\sum_{r<N}f(S^{\star}_r)}{\sum_{r<N}h(S^{\star}_r)}\xrightarrow{N\to\infty}\dfrac{\pi^{\star}(f)}{\pi^{\star}(h)}=\int_{\mathsf{S}}g\,d\pi\) a.s.
  • (조건부→실현) \(\sum_{r<N}(F_r-f(S^{\star}_r)),\ \sum_{r<N}(m_r-h(S^{\star}_r))\) 평균 0 마팅게일, 증분 \((\ast)\)·\(\leq D\) \(\implies o(N)\) a.s. \(\bigl(\because \sum_{r<N}h\geq N\bigr)\).
  • (라운드→스텝) \(t_N:=\sum_{r<N}m_r\), \(\ \sum_{r<N}F_r=\sum_{k=0}^{t_N-1}g(S_k)\ \implies\ \dfrac1{t_N}\sum_{k=0}^{t_N-1}g(S_k)\xrightarrow{N}\int_{\mathsf{S}}g\,d\pi\) a.s.
  • (부분라운드) \(\forall t\,(t_N\leq t<t_{N+1})\): 누락분 \(\leq\sum_{q<m_N}|g(S_{t_N+q})|=o(t)\ \land\ t/t_N\to1\) \(\implies\) 극한 \(\forall t\) 유지.

\(\therefore\ g=\mathbf{1}_A\)로 두면 \(\pi\) = per-step occupation 법칙 = \(\{S_t\}\)의 \(\exists!\,\)불변확률. \(\blacksquare\)

Theorem (balanced-regime convergence)

Assume the standing setting (connected \(\mathcal{G}\), canonical degree-proportional \(\boldsymbol{\mu}_0\)) and the single Tier 2 hypothesis (A3) of §2: \((\mathcal{G}, \boldsymbol{\mu}_0, b, T_{\max})\) satisfies the \(\ell\)-round Foster drift (Assumption Foster). Then there exists a deterministic matrix \(C = [c_{ij}]_{i,j \in \mathsf{V}}\) with \(c_{ij} = c_{ij}(\mathcal{G}, \boldsymbol{\mu}_0, b, T_{\max}) \geq 0\) such that

\[\frac{SD^2_{ij}(t)}{t} \;\xrightarrow{t \to \infty}\; c_{ij} \qquad \text{a.s.,}\]

and the limit \(c_{ij}\) is independent of the initial signal \(\mathbf{y}\). The balanced occupation \(\rho_i = 1/n\) (\(i = 1, \ldots, n\)) is not assumed; under (A3) it follows from the stationary law.

Proof. 관측량 \(g(S):=(h(v_i)-h(v_j))^2\) (\(\mathring{\mathbf{h}}\)의 함수, \(\because\ h(v_1)\) 상쇄), \(\dfrac{SD^2_{ij}(t)}{t}=\dfrac1t\sum_{k=0}^{t}g(S_k)\). Goal: 이 평균의 a.s. 수렴(유한·\(\mathbf{y}\)-무관). (\(g\geq0\implies\) 극한 \(\geq0\); 종단 \(k=t\) 항은 \(/t\) 후 무시.) 네 Step으로 환원한다.

Step 1 — per-step 주장을 한-라운드 두 의무로.

Goal: \(\ \text{정리}\ \Longleftarrow\ \text{(I) }P^{\star}\ \text{양 Harris},\ \exists!\,\pi^{\star}(\mathbf{y}\text{-무관})\ \land\ \text{(II) }\mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}[\Phi]<\infty.\)

  • (적분가능성) \(1\leq m_r\leq T_{\max}+1\ \land\ \text{(II)}\ \implies\ g(S_{t_r+q})\leq C_g(1+\Phi(S^{\star}_r))\ \implies\ \mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}\bigl[\sum_{q<m_r}|g|\bigr]\leq(T_{\max}{+}1)C_g(1+\mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}[\Phi])<\infty\ \ [(\ast)].\)
  • (리프트 적용) \(\text{(I)}\land\text{(II)}\ \overset{\text{Lemma 5}}{\implies}\ \dfrac{SD^2_{ij}(t)}{t}=\dfrac1t\sum_{k=0}^{t}g(S_k)\xrightarrow{t\to\infty}\int_{\mathsf{S}}g\,d\pi=:c_{ij}\) a.s.
  • (성질 확인) \(c_{ij}<\infty\ \land\ \mathbf{y}\text{-무관}\ (\because\ \text{(I)})\).

\(\therefore\ \text{정리}\ \Longleftarrow\ \text{(I)}\land\text{(II)}.\)

Step 2 — 한-라운드 의무를 \(\ell\)-라운드 스켈레톤 \(Q:=(P^{\star})^{\ell}\)로.

Goal: \(\ \text{(I)}\land\text{(II)}\ \Longleftarrow\ Q\ \text{양 Harris},\ \exists!\,\pi^{\star}(\mathbf{y}\text{-무관}),\ \mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}[\Phi]<\infty.\)

  • (불변성 이전) \((\pi^{\star}P^{\star})Q=\pi^{\star}(P^{\star})^{\ell+1}=(\pi^{\star}Q)P^{\star}=\pi^{\star}P^{\star}\ \implies\ \pi^{\star}P^{\star}\ Q\text{-불변}\ \overset{\exists!}{\implies}\ \pi^{\star}P^{\star}=\pi^{\star}\ \implies\ \pi^{\star}\ P^{\star}\text{-불변}.\)
  • (역방향) \(P^{\star}\text{-불변}\ \implies\ Q\text{-불변}\ \implies\ =\pi^{\star}\) (유일성).
  • (Harris 하강) \(A\ \text{by }Q=(P^{\star})^{\ell}\ \text{i.o.}\ \implies\ A\ \text{by }P^{\star}\ \text{i.o.}\)

\(\therefore\ \text{(I) for }P^{\star}\ \text{성립};\quad \text{(II)}=\pi^{\star}\text{만의 성질이라 불변}.\)

Step 3 — \(Q\)의 세 성질을 세 가설로 (\(V_{\mathrm{Foster}}(t_r):=\Phi(t_r)^2\)).

Goal: \(\ Q\text{의 성질}\ \Longleftarrow\ \) 아래 (H1)(H2)(H3):

  • (H1) \(\psi\text{-기약}\land\text{비주기}\);
  • (H2) petite \(\mathsf{C}_0\);
  • (H3) \(\mathbb{E}[\Delta_\ell\Phi^2\mid s^{\star}]\leq-\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2n^{3/2}}\Phi^{3/2}+C_{\mathrm{cpt}}\mathbf{1}_{\mathsf{C}_0}.\)

그러면 차근차근:

  • (양 Harris) \(\text{(H3)}=\text{Lemma 1 드리프트},\ \varphi(x)=\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2n^{3/2}}x^{3/4}\,(\nearrow,\to\infty),\ \text{petite }\mathsf{C}_0\ \overset{\text{Lemma 1}}{\implies}\ Q\ \text{양 Harris},\ \exists!\,\pi^{\star}\,(\mathbf{y}\text{-무관},\ \because\ \text{커널이 }\mathring{\mathbf{h}}\text{만 의존}).\)
  • (모멘트) \(\overset{\text{Lemma 2},\ f=\varphi(V_{\mathrm{Foster}})=\frac{\varepsilon}{2n^{3/2}}\Phi^{3/2}}{\implies}\ \mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}[\Phi^{3/2}]<\infty\ \implies\ \mathbb{E}_{\pi^{\star}}[\Phi]<\infty\ \ [\text{(II)}].\)

Step 4 — (H1)(H2)(H3) 해소 (§5.1–§5.3; “\((P^{\star})^{\ell}\)은 petite” 보조정리가 모든 거듭제곱에 적용).

Goal: 세 가설을 이미 증명된 §5.1–§5.3 결과로 각각 닫기.

  • (H1) \((P^{\star})^{\ell}\ \psi\text{-기약}(\psi=\nu)\land\text{비주기}\ \overset{\text{twin minorisation}}{\Longleftarrow}\ \text{"}\ell\text{-skeleton petite" 보조정리}.\)
  • (H2) \(\mathsf{C}_0=\{M(s^{\star})\leq M_0\}\ \text{small}\ (\therefore\ \text{petite})\ \text{for }(P^{\star})^{\ell}.\)
  • (H3) \(=\ \Phi^2\ \text{Foster 드리프트 보조정리 for }(P^{\star})^{\ell}.\)

\(\therefore\ \text{잔여 의무 }\varnothing. \qquad\blacksquare\)
