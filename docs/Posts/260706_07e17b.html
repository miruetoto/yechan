기호주의 모드(대표 지시). 함의·근거를 전부 화살표(⟹/⟺) 골격으로 노출. 산문화는 검증·승인 후. 원본: 에이 편집용 hst_AAA.tex §5.2 (07-06 02:48 재구성본).

기호(Claim 1~5): 낙하벡터 \(\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}\in[0,b]^{nL}\), fall map \(\mathbf{A}^{\sharp}\)·유사역 \((\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}\)·커널기저 \(\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp}\), fall shift \(\mathbf{a}^{\sharp}\), 자유좌표 \(\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\), Jacobian \(J\), slice floor \(m_0\) [Claim 5], chamber \(\mathsf{U}\), \(s^{\star}\in\mathsf{C}_0\). 비교 \(\mathsf{A}=\{a=(i,j,\rho)\}\), 계수 \(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\in\{0,\pm1\}^{nL}\) fall order(시간순) — \(\rho'\)번째 성분 = \(\rho'\)번째 낙하(\(\rho'\le\rho\)이고 \(v_i\)면 \(+1\)·\(v_j\)면 \(-1\), 아니면 \(0\)), gap \(=\Delta_{ij}(\mathring{\mathbf{h}}(s^{\star}))+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\cdot\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}\).

Claim 6 (branch pick floor)

진술. \(\mathbf{b}^{\flat}=\mathbf{0}\)·cyclic schedule 조건. 모든 비교가 resolved(strictly-lower 결과가 event 위 상수; exact tie = “not strictly lower” 결정론)인 event 위 ⟹ resolved pattern이 매 flow step의 strictly-lower set 결정 ⟹ pattern 일치 branch \(\amalg\) 를 walker 가 확률 \(\ge(1/n)^{2nL(n-1)}\) 로 실현.

증명.

\[ \text{매 step move law} = \text{strictly-lower 이웃 위}\ (\text{resolved outcome}) \] \[ \text{각 라운드 strictly-decreasing path } \le n-1\ \text{flows} < (\ge n\ \text{허용}) \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{threshold 안 binding} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{itinerary feasible} \] \[ \le nL(n-1)\ \text{flow step (다중 strictly-lower)},\ \text{각 degree-weighted pick atom} \ge 1/n^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{곱} \ge (1/n)^{2nL(n-1)}.\ \blacksquare \]

Claim 7 (fiber / 퇴화 dichotomy / tie-tube)

진술. fiber \(\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}=(\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}\mathbf{a}^{\sharp}+\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp}\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\) ⟹ 각 gap \(\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\)의 아핀, 기울기 \((\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp})^{\top}\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\). 퇴화 = 기울기 \(\mathbf{0}\)(fiber 상수), 비퇴화 \(\mathsf{A}_{\mathrm{nd}}:=\{a:(\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp})^{\top}\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\ne\mathbf{0}\}\). row-space obs: \(\operatorname{Range}((\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{\top})=\{\)노드그룹 상수, 그룹합 \(0\}\). \(\kappa_{\mathsf{U}}:=\min_{i\ne j}\inf_{\mathbf{u}\in\overline{\mathsf{U}}}|\Delta_{ij}(\mathbf{u})|>0\). 아래 (I)(II).

(I) 퇴화 dichotomy.

\[ (\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp})^{\top}\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a=\mathbf{0} \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\perp\ker\mathbf{A}^{\sharp} \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\in\operatorname{Range}((\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{\top}) \overset{\text{row-space}}{=\!=\!=} \{\text{노드그룹 상수, 합}=0\} \] \[ +\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\ (v_i\text{군}\,{+1}\,/\,v_j\text{군}\,{-1}\,/\,\text{else}\,0) \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{각 노드 all-or-none} \;\Longrightarrow\; \boldsymbol{\alpha}_a=\mathbf{0}\ (\text{fall 무관, Claim 9 regionwise 처리})\ \ \text{또는}\ \ \text{final-equality} \] \[ \text{final-equality}: \Delta_{ij}(\mathring{\mathbf{h}}(s^{\star}))+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\cdot\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}=\Delta_{ij}(\mathbf{u}) \;\Longrightarrow\; |\Delta_{ij}(\mathbf{u})|\ge\kappa_{\mathsf{U}}\ (\ast).\ \blacksquare \]

(II) tie-tube. (목표: \(\bigl|\{\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}:\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}\in[0,b]^{nL},\ \min_{a\in\mathsf{A}_{\mathrm{nd}}}|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\cdot\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}+\Delta_{ij}(\mathring{\mathbf{h}})|\le\delta\}\bigr|\le C_{\mathrm{tie}}\delta\).)

\[ \mathsf{A}_{\mathrm{nd}}\ \text{유한} \Rightarrow \lambda_\ast:=\min_{\mathsf{A}_{\mathrm{nd}}}\|(\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp})^{\top}\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\|>0;\quad \text{비교} = \mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\text{-아핀, 기울기}\ (\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp})^{\top}\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a,\ \|\cdot\|\ge\lambda_\ast \] \[ \;\Longrightarrow\; \{|\cdot|\le\delta\}\ \text{길이}\le 2\delta/\lambda_\ast,\ \text{slice 지름}\le b\sqrt{nL} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{유계단면 적분}+\text{유한합}\le C_{\mathrm{tie}}\delta.\ \blacksquare \]

Claim 8 (경계 cost / robust core)

(I) 경계. (목표: \(\bigl|\{\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}:\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}\in[0,b]^{nL},\ \min_j b^{\sharp}_j\le\delta\ \text{or}\ \min_j(b-b^{\sharp}_j)\le\delta\}\bigr|\le C_{\mathrm{bdry}}\delta\).)

\[ \text{면}\ \{b^{\sharp}_j=0/b\}:\ \text{아핀}\ \mathbf{e}_j\cdot((\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}\mathbf{a}^{\sharp}+\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp}\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}),\ (\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp})^{\top}\mathbf{e}_j\ne\mathbf{0}\ (\because\ \text{아니면}\ \mathbf{e}_j\in\operatorname{Range}((\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{\top}),\ \text{row-space 모순}) \] \[ \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{같은 slicing},\ 2nL\ \text{면} \;\Longrightarrow\; \le C_{\mathrm{bdry}}\delta.\ \blacksquare \]

(II) robust core. \((C_{\mathrm{tie}}+C_{\mathrm{bdry}})\delta\le m_0/2,\ \delta\le\kappa_{\mathsf{U}}\) 조건 하: \[ \mathsf{R}_\delta(s^{\star}):=\{\delta\le b^{\sharp}_j\le b-\delta\ \forall j\}\cap\bigcap_{a\in\mathsf{A}_{\mathrm{nd}}}\{|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_a\cdot\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}+\Delta_{ij}(\mathring{\mathbf{h}})|\ge\delta\} \] \[ \text{전체 slice}=m_0\ [\text{Claim 5}],\ \text{도려낸 tube}\le(C_{\mathrm{tie}}+C_{\mathrm{bdry}})\delta\le m_0/2\ [\text{Claim 7(II)}+(\text{I})] \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{잔존}\ge m_0/2=:m_\ast \] \[ \text{core 위: 비퇴화}\ge\delta,\ \text{final-equality no tie}\ [\text{Claim 7(I)}],\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_a=\mathbf{0}\ \text{상수(no cut)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{fall-side pattern well-defined} \;\Longrightarrow\; \mathsf{R}_\delta=\textstyle\bigsqcup_{\amalg}\mathsf{R}_{\delta,\amalg}.\ \blacksquare \]

Claim 9 (transport — off \(\mathbf{b}^{\flat}=\mathbf{0}\) branch 유지)

진술. \(\mathbf{b}^{\flat}\in[0,\varepsilon]^{nLT_{\max}},\ \mathbf{u}\in\mathsf{U},\ \amalg\) ⟹ slice \(\{\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}:(\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}(\mathbf{u}-\mathring{\mathbf{h}})+\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp}\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\in\mathsf{R}_{\delta,\amalg}\}\)의 모든 \(\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\)가 \((\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}(\mathbf{u}-\mathring{\mathbf{h}}-\mathbf{A}^{\flat}_{\amalg}\mathbf{b}^{\flat})+\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp}\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\in[0,b]^{nL}\)·branch \(\amalg\) 실현.

증명. (목표: 모든 비교를 margin 절반 미만으로만 움직이면 됨.)

\[ \text{단일}\ \Gamma<\infty:\ \{\|\mathbf{A}^{\flat}_{\amalg}\mathbf{b}^{\flat}\|,\ \|(\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}\mathbf{A}^{\flat}_{\amalg}\mathbf{b}^{\flat}\|,\ \text{비교의}\ \mathbf{b}^{\flat}\text{-감도}\}\le\Gamma\varepsilon;\quad \varepsilon\le\min\{b/(8nT_{\max}),\ \delta/(4\Gamma),\ \kappa_{\mathsf{U}}/(4\Gamma)\} \] \[ \mathbf{b}^{\sharp}\mapsto\widehat{\mathbf{b}}^{\sharp}:=\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}-(\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}\mathbf{A}^{\flat}_{\amalg}\mathbf{b}^{\flat} \;\Longrightarrow\; \mathbf{A}^{\sharp}\widehat{\mathbf{b}}^{\sharp}=\mathbf{u}-\mathring{\mathbf{h}}-\mathbf{A}^{\flat}_{\amalg}\mathbf{b}^{\flat};\ (\dagger)\ \text{좌표로}\ \mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\ \textbf{불변}\ (\text{target만 이동}) \] \[ \text{shift}\le\Gamma\varepsilon\le\delta/4 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{비퇴화}\ \le\delta/2\ \text{이동, 부호 유지};\quad \text{final-equality}\ \ge\kappa_{\mathsf{U}}/2\ [\text{Claim 7(I)}] \] \[ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_a=\mathbf{0}:\ \text{fall 무관} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{transport 안 움직임},\ \text{outcome}=(s^{\star},\mathbf{b}^{\flat})\ \text{함수·region 상수},\ \amalg\ \text{가 그 region 일치로 선택} \] \[ \;\Longrightarrow\; \widehat{\mathbf{b}}^{\sharp}\in[0,b]^{nL}\cdot\text{branch}\ \amalg\ \text{유지} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{slice 측도}\ge\text{원래, uniform in}\ \mathbf{b}^{\flat}.\ \blacksquare \]

Claim 10 (discrete scaffold)

진술. \(\forall s^{\star}\in\mathsf{C}_0\) branch pin 가능·uniform floor: cyclic schedule + non-fall \(\le\varepsilon\) + fall \(\in\mathsf{R}_{\delta,\amalg}\)(\(\mathbf{b}^{\flat}\) region 일치, Claim 9) 조건 ⟹ walker \(\amalg\) 실현 \(\ge(1/n)^{2nL(n-1)}\); 두 조건 event \(\ge\mu_{\min}^{nL},\ (\varepsilon/b)^{nLT_{\max}}\) ⟹ scaffold \(\le\mu_{\min}^{nL}(1/n)^{2nL(n-1)}(\varepsilon/b)^{nLT_{\max}}\).

증명.

\[ \text{cyclic schedule}\ (\textstyle\prod_i\boldsymbol{\mu}_0(v_i))^L\ge\mu_{\min}^{nL}\ [\text{iid}\ \boldsymbol{\mu}_0,\ \text{snowfall A}];\quad \text{non-fall smallness}\ge(\varepsilon/b)^{nLT_{\max}}\ [\text{step당}\ \varepsilon/b] \] \[ \mathsf{R}_{\delta,\amalg}\ \text{위 fall-dependent 비교 strictly signed}\ [\text{Claim 8(II)}] + \boldsymbol{\alpha}_a=\mathbf{0}\ \text{non-fall region 상수 resolved}\ [\text{Claim 9}] \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{모든 비교 resolved} \] \[ \overset{\text{Claim 6}}{\Longrightarrow} \text{walker}\ \amalg\ \ge(1/n)^{2nL(n-1)},\ \text{uniform in}\ s^{\star}.\ \blacksquare \]

Assembly

목표. \((P^{\star})^{nL}(s^{\star},\mathsf{B}\times\{v_n\})\ge\eta\,|\mathsf{B}|/|\mathsf{U}|\) ⟹ \(\mathsf{C}_0\) small.

\[ \text{endpoint}=\text{terminal fall}\ (X_{t_{r+nL}}=v_n,\ \text{no subsequent flow});\quad \mathring{\mathbf{h}}(s^{\star})\longmapsto\mathring{\mathbf{h}}(s^{\star})+\mathbf{A}^{\sharp}\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}+\mathbf{A}^{\flat}_{\amalg}\mathbf{b}^{\flat}\ (\text{후자 Claim 9로 branch pin}) \]

scenario suffices: \(\mathsf{U}\) 안착 [Claim 3(I)(II)] \(\wedge\) branch \(\amalg\) uniform floor [Claim 10] \(\wedge\) fall density floor [Claim 4] \(\wedge\) non-fall 안 깸 [Claim 9]. scenario: cyclic falls(terminal \(v_n\)) + \(\amalg\)(\(\mathbf{b}^{\flat}\) 고정) + \(\mathbf{b}^{\sharp}\in\mathsf{R}_{\delta,\amalg}\) + non-fall \(\le\varepsilon\) ⟹ walker \(\to v_n\), 높이\(\in\mathsf{B}\) ⟺ \(\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{B}}\).

\[ \rho_{\mathbf{b}^{\flat}}(\mathbf{u})=\tfrac{J}{b^{nL}}\sum_{\amalg}\bigl|\{\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}:(\mathbf{A}^{\sharp})^{+}(\mathbf{u}-\mathring{\mathbf{h}}-\mathbf{A}^{\flat}_{\amalg}\mathbf{b}^{\flat})+\mathbf{Z}^{\sharp}\mathbf{z}^{\sharp}\in\mathsf{R}_{\delta,\amalg}\}\bigr| \overset{\text{Claim 8,9}}{\ge} m_\ast J/b^{nL}=:c_\ast>0\ \text{on}\ \mathsf{U} \] \[ \begin{aligned} (P^{\star})^{nL}(s^{\star},\mathsf{B}\times\{v_n\}) &\ge \mu_{\min}^{nL}(1/n)^{2nL(n-1)}\!\int_{[0,\varepsilon]^{nLT_{\max}}}\! b^{-nLT_{\max}}\!\int_{\mathsf{B}}\rho_{\mathbf{b}^{\flat}}(\mathbf{u})\,d\mathbf{u}\,d\mathbf{b}^{\flat} &&(\because\mathsf{G}_{\mathsf{B}},\ \text{Claim 10})\\ &\ge \mu_{\min}^{nL}(1/n)^{2nL(n-1)}(\varepsilon/b)^{nLT_{\max}}c_\ast|\mathsf{B}| &&(\because\rho\ge c_\ast,\ \textstyle\int\mathbf{b}^{\flat})\\ &= \eta\,|\mathsf{B}|/|\mathsf{U}| &&(\eta:=\mu_{\min}^{nL}(1/n)^{2nL(n-1)}(\varepsilon/b)^{nLT_{\max}}c_\ast|\mathsf{U}|>0) \end{aligned} \] \[ \;\Longrightarrow\; (P^{\star})^{nL}(s^{\star},\cdot)\ge\eta\,\nu,\ \ \nu=\mathrm{Unif}(\mathsf{U})\otimes\delta_{v_n} \;\Longrightarrow\; \mathsf{C}_0\ \text{small}.\ \blacksquare \]

Snowfall A 필수. 각 낙하 Lebesgue 밀도 ⟹ fall density floor. Snowfall B ⟹ 라운드 상수 ⟹ \(nL\) 좌표가 라운드당 스칼라로 붕괴 ⟹ image Lebesgue-null in \(\mathbb{R}^{n-1}\) ⟹ floor 실패.
