연구 ▷ HST ▷ Pushforward Density of \(\Delta_1\varrho\)

Author

유진

Published

May 24, 2026

\(\Delta_1\varrho := (\varrho_2-\varrho_1,\ldots,\varrho_n-\varrho_1)\)은 HST 초기 설정에서 상대 높이 벡터다. 절대 높이 \(\varrho_i\)는 시간이 지날수록 무한히 커지지만, 상대 높이는 bounded region에 머문다. Lemma 4는 이 벡터의 pushforward density가 특정 영역 안에서 아래로 균일하게 bound됨을 보인다.

Lemma 4 (Pushforward density). Let \(\varrho_1,\ldots,\varrho_n\overset{\text{iid}}{\sim}\text{Unif}(0,b)\) and \(\Delta_1\varrho:=(\varrho_2-\varrho_1,\ldots,\varrho_n-\varrho_1)\in\mathbb{R}^{n-1}\). The density \(f_{\text{pdf}}\) of \(\Delta_1\varrho\) satisfies \[f_{\text{pdf}}(\delta\varrho)\;\geq\;\frac{b^{1-n}}{2}\qquad\text{whenever }\|\delta\varrho\|_\infty<\frac{b}{4}.\]

Proof. We use two claims.

Claim 1. \(f_{\text{pdf}}(\delta\varrho)=b^{-n}\,\ell(\delta\varrho)\), where \(\ell(\delta\varrho):=\bigl|[0,b]\cap\bigcap_{i=1}^{n-1}[-\delta\varrho_i,\,b-\delta\varrho_i]\bigr|\).

Claim 2. \(\|\delta\varrho\|_\infty<b/4\;\Rightarrow\;\ell(\delta\varrho)>b/2\).

Assuming the claims, \[f_{\text{pdf}}(\delta\varrho)=b^{-n}\,\ell(\delta\varrho)>b^{-n}\cdot\frac{b}{2}=\frac{b^{1-n}}{2}.\] It remains to prove the claims.

Proof of Claim 1. The map \(\varrho\mapsto(\varrho_1,\,\Delta_1\varrho)\) is lower-triangular with Jacobian \(1\), so the joint density of \((\varrho_1,\delta\varrho)\) is \(b^{-n}\cdot\mathbf{1}[\varrho\in[0,b]^n]\). The inverse gives \(\varrho_{i+1}=\varrho_1+\delta\varrho_i\), so \[\varrho\in[0,b]^n\;\Leftrightarrow\;\varrho_1\in[0,b]\,\cap\,\bigcap_{i=1}^{n-1}[-\delta\varrho_i,\,b-\delta\varrho_i]\;=:\;\mathcal{I}(\delta\varrho).\] Marginalizing over \(\varrho_1\): \[f_{\text{pdf}}(\delta\varrho)=\int_\mathbb{R}b^{-n}\,\mathbf{1}[\varrho_1\in\mathcal{I}(\delta\varrho)]\,d\varrho_1=b^{-n}\,\ell(\delta\varrho).\]

Proof of Claim 2. Write \(\mathcal{I}(\delta\varrho)=[c^-,\,c^+]\) where \[c^+=b-\max(0,\,\max_i\,\delta\varrho_i),\qquad c^-=\max(0,\,-\min_i\,\delta\varrho_i).\] Under \(\|\delta\varrho\|_\infty<b/4\): \[\max_i\,\delta\varrho_i<\frac{b}{4}\;\Rightarrow\;c^+>\frac{3b}{4},\qquad -\min_i\,\delta\varrho_i<\frac{b}{4}\;\Rightarrow\;c^-<\frac{b}{4}.\] Therefore \(\ell(\delta\varrho)=c^+-c^->b/2\). \(\square\)

해설. 핵심 아이디어는 \(\varrho\mapsto(\varrho_1,\Delta_1\varrho)\)로 좌표를 바꾸면 \(\varrho_1\) 하나에만 적분이 남는다는 것이다. \(b/4\) 조건은 \(n\)개의 uniform 구간 \([0,b]\), \([-\delta\varrho_i,\,b-\delta\varrho_i]\)의 공통 부분이 길이 \(b/2\) 이상임을 보장한다. \(n\)이 커질수록 하한 \(b^{1-n}/2\)은 작아지지만, 영역 \(\|\delta\varrho\|_\infty<b/4\) 안에서 density가 uniformly positive하다는 성질은 보존된다.
